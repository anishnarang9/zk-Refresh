8. **Security assumptions, risks and limits.** Directories are private 700, regular evidence/table files 600 and immutable executable copies 700. Only public synthetic inputs were used. The table loader opens a single descriptor, validates private owner/type/size, hashes the same bounded received canonical bytes while decoding, and enforces exact vector length, EOF and supplied SHA. Full authenticated weight-table consistency belongs the material/import owner; the cross engine checks length and weight zero, not an O(N) recomputation each call. Structural preflights include simultaneous product/numerator/remainder/Newton/FFT/output storage but do not promise OOM recovery, usable allocator size, OS RSS or constant-time behavior. Observer callbacks receive only boundaries and are outside engine allocation/panic guarantees. Diagnostic allocator tracks requested bytes, not usable heap or RSS; thread stacks/runtime/library retention have separate effects. RSS/storage limits are sampled and can miss short spikes, including brief batch-inversion scratch in the four-sample preparation run. Reports count positive logical file growth and exclude post-run evidence copies. One public seed/layout and deliberately chosen overlap/disjoint shapes do not establish actual credential crossover, adversarial worst-case performance or an asymptotic empirical claim. No seven-transform full baseline changed.
