## Approved engineering representation

`ReconstructionMaterial` shares one exact-package public UK plus forward/reverse kernel transforms and root powers through Arc. Its structural package/table scan is performed once at preparation; updates use the cached count. Public table construction/FFT work and its retained capacities are separate from private reconstruction quota.

`DynamicPreparation::adopt` consumes an opaque T2 Anchor and moves its 2N projections/2N rows without copying them. Requested workspace and mark capacities are checked before reservation and actual capacities afterward. Two group/four scalar buffers and the mark words are initialized through bounded zero-append actions; initialization costs 6N+ceil(10N/64). Initial buffers adopted cost 4N transferred cells. `finish` refuses incomplete preparation. No external alias can observe recycled holder buffers.

Private pool ownership is fixed at four G1 vectors and six Fr vectors. Physical slot IDs stay stable; logical active and builder roles move at promotion. Frozen inputs reference active row slots and immutable sorted normalized overlay pairs. Every rebuild materializes rows through a monotone cursor. Maps are immutable Arc<Vec<(usize,Fr)>> allocations; merges combine equal rows and drop field zero. Capacity accounting distinguishes allocation identity, not just Arc reference count. Only current assignment is retained through Arc; the frozen generation/token/distance and exact immutable row source/overlay define the reconstruction input, so no separate dense snapshot assignment is retained.

Reconstruction actions are the reviewed header, materialize, prepare, four forward streams, pointwise, four inverse streams, scale and integrate phases. `W=1+19N+4N*log2(N)`; group scalar calls are instrumented at the actual multiplication sites and total 2N*log2(N)+10N. The first implementation executes elementary actions serially. Four Rayon threads remain available to inherited preparation/full backend work; no parallel reconstruction or cached GLV integration is claimed.

`DynamicState::begin_update` fully validates the exact target and its public values, stages immutable maps, performs cap checks, samples both caller randomizers and stages the exact raw correction/ordinary proof before pool mutation. `advance_update` retains a private pending Root and one physical first-touch journal across every chunk until the whole target commits. Binding/phase reads return Busy during a pending target. Committed Root remains untouched; cancellation/error restores every actual old cell and discards staged metadata. No arithmetic, entropy or allocation occurs during cell restoration. Tuple writes preregister all their preimages before their first write. Mark cleanup visits only journaled indices.

Journal values and encoded physical indices use separate vectors; 96-byte G1, 32-byte Fr and 8-byte usize sizes are checked by the small gate. Planned bounds are U_G=min(4N,2K), U_F=min(6N,2K), with 104U_G+40U_F payload/index bytes plus marks. Capacity growth is reserved in replacement vectors without changing retained buffers on a failed reservation. Temporary coexistence of old/new capacity is included in peak preflight. Final journal allowance is also constrained by remaining private/global byte budgets, so a failed reservation cannot install oversized retained capacity. Retained capacity remains charged after successful mark cleanup.

`DynamicConfig` independently bounds domain/assignment/matrix/delta work through T2 Limits, normalized row support, total quadratic pair visits across both grouped corrections, transaction actions, journal bytes, private structural bytes and global structural bytes. Private preflight includes pool, journal, old/staged distinct maps, assignments, metadata, row deltas, cross coefficient scratch and statement output; full fallback also includes a conservative dense quotient/MSM scratch bound. Global adds shared public package/index/UK/kernel/root capacities once. These count vector capacities and explicit struct storage; allocator metadata, Arc allocation headers, native stacks, backend transient allocations and RSS are not identified by these structural numbers. Runtime ceilings are independently sampled by the bounded runner.

Current binding starts from the exact immutable Anchor commitment. Each accepted transition derives a SHA-256 domain-separated token binding every PackageIdentity digest, domain, credential/action, old/new generation, prior token, length-prefixed normalized delta/public values and constant-size canonical raw components/randomizers. No N-sized state hash is performed by an ordinary transition. Tokens are identity commitments; they establish neither application freshness nor authenticated durable persistence.

Scheduler stores snapshot accepted-distance counters separately from generations. Lifetime C never refunds reversed numerical changes; t uses F(u)=floor(W*u/b). At a crossing, complete old builder then promote its exact frozen rows/q and pending corrections through the whole target. New builder freezes the actual target with empty pending maps; header is paid even at e=0, while overshoot credit F(e) subtracts its paid header. Every recycled write stays in the same journal. InitialReadyAlias preserves existing active slots and does not increment distinct-reconstruction count. Named zero delta advances generation/token and obtains fresh proof entropy while preserving t/C/maps/maintenance.

Eligible distance/row/pair/action/memory caps choose configured Reject or explicitly paid FullOnly. Identity, validation and entropy errors never become silent fallback. Successful full fallback retires pool/projection/builder states, retains only current assignment/raw proof/binding and lifetime C, and resets t. Explicit `full_for_binding` validates the package-bound assignment and performs paid full capture for new credential/action context. Re-entering dynamics requires a fresh matching Anchor and paid preparation. No full proof is mislabeled DynamicReady.

Future T6 schema must bind version, complete package/domain/credential/action, current generation/token/C/t/status, physical role IDs, buffer lengths, field/group values, builder phase/cursors/source/frozen overlay namespace and raw state. Parent chose compressed canonical point encoding: persisted points are mathematically preserved, while exact projective XYZ coordinates are required only for same-process rollback.
