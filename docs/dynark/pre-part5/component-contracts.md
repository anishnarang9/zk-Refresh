# Pre-Part 5 component contracts, development scope v1

October 15, 2026. This contract governs the local components prepared for Part 5 in this repository. It preserves the original circuit, `.zkey`, generated verifier, program and historical evidence. Changed identities receive separate versioned packages. The current program issues a receipt only; Part 5 will add the protected work-request effect in `program/src/lib.rs` and its caller in `harness/src/solana.rs`.

## Package and statement identity

The accepted unchanged synthetic circuit is `credential-v1`, BN254/Groth16, Original reduction, six instance coordinates including one, and five ordered public inputs `[root, issuerHash, notBefore, notAfter, actionHash]`. Its R1CS SHA-256 is `ff4ee937061ee60e4d4043f1213ff562f72e0828986462e8ebbec8cdfabb326d`; retained Original public-package identity is `57cea7ff53a711f8fbb2e8f256a21a852d087564c49df70b6065c6994765c0d5`. These are development identities, not proof of a production ceremony or original `.zkey` compatibility.

`credential-v2-dev` is an additive relation for the narrow, controlled-CA development profile. Its identity is the hash of a canonical manifest containing the exact Circom sources/includes, compiler version, generated profile, R1CS, witness executable, public schema, reduction, curve, proving key, verification key and setup transcript identity. No artifact is admitted until all manifest digests are measured, its matching development setup is qualified, and ordinary full and exposed refreshed proofs verify. The five-public-input shape may remain, but it has a **new schema identity** because the statement and action encoding change. V1 dependency mappings and keys never transfer by signal names or R1CS similarity.

The development profile uses locally controlled RSA-2048/e65537/SHA-256/PKCS#1 v1.5 issuer and holder keys. It accepts one fully specified canonical DER v3 template with bounded TBS and whole-certificate lengths, a single constrained signed opaque 16-byte organization identifier, 32-byte group identifier, dedicated application-purpose EKU, digitalSignature key usage, and end-entity basicConstraints. All eligibility bytes, DER structure, algorithm bytes, holder modulus, dates and extension presence/order are constrained as signed TBS bytes; the signature and complete EMSA encoding are checked in the circuit. Duplicate or unrecognized extensions, unsupported critical extensions, alternate algorithms and encodings fail admission. The development template supports valid UTCTime years 2000–2049 with inclusive certificate validity endpoints. GeneralizedTime requires a separately versioned supported template and is outside this first development package. Development OIDs are explicitly marked test-only and recorded in the generated profile; production issuer OIDs are an external qualification input. Administrator enrollment validates the controlled CA and its chain outside the proof, while the circuit verifies the enrolled issuing key's signature over the supported TBS.

The public `issuerHash` binds the enrolled RSA modulus. Enrollment separately binds that hash to a unique issuer version and enabled policy; disabling or rotating it invalidates affected client state. A local signer receives the exact 32-byte canonical `actionHash` field encoding and a declared SHA-256/PKCS#1 v1.5 mechanism, and returns a 256-byte RSA signature without exporting a real holder key. A synthetic signer is permitted in tests.

## Canonical action v2

The action preimage is the following byte sequence, with no implicit serialization, text normalization or optional fields:

`ASCII("DYNARK-WORK-REQUEST") || u8(2) || network_genesis[32] || program[32] || registry[32] || policy_id[32] || policy_version:u32_be || circuit_id[32] || nonce[32] || recipient[32] || request_type:u16_be || payload_len:u32_be || SHA256(payload)[32] || account_count:u8 || account_meta[account_count] || deadline_unix:i64_be`.

Each account meta is `pubkey[32] || flags:u8` in execution order; only bit 0 (signer) and bit 1 (writable) are valid. The development queue profile bounds payload to 1,024 bytes and account count to 16; exact request type and effect semantics are versioned with the program. All integer encodings are canonical big-endian; deadline is mandatory and positive. `actionHash = SHA256(preimage) mod Fr`, encoded as a canonical 32-byte big-endian BN254 scalar. The holder signs SHA-256 of that exact 32-byte field encoding, matching the circuit's holder-signature statement. Collision resistance is bounded by the scalar field size. The Part 5 program must recompute the preimage and `actionHash` from actual instruction data and account metas before executing the request. A supplied digest is never sufficient. The nonce is consumed atomically with a successful protected effect; failure leaves it available. The action bytes remain stable across root-only retries, and a changed request invalidates reuse.

## Registry, issuer status and currentness

V1's leaf and its domain constant `20261009` remain unchanged. V2 development occupied leaves use `Poseidon(5)` over `[20261015, SHA256(TBS)_high128, SHA256(TBS)_low128, slot, active]`, where active is exactly one for an eligible leaf and zero after revocation; an unused slot is the zero field element. The v2 tree depth is 10. Stable private credential identity is `(issuer enrollment ID, issuer version, SHA256(TBS))`; slot and slot-allocation epoch are registry metadata, not interchangeable identity. Reuse of a slot requires prior deactivation, a higher allocation epoch and a different authenticated identity. Old identities retain tombstones and cannot regain membership through replacement.

The development source is an authenticated signed issuer-status feed, separate from the registry publisher. A publisher update binds network, registry, publisher key/version, policy version, source authority and observation sequence/digest, predecessor version/root/envelope digest, new version/root, canonical bounded delta or snapshot digest, and issuance/expiry times. The client admits a delta only from the exact authenticated predecessor; a gap requires a fresh authenticated snapshot checked against the authoritative current root. Apply updates privately, recompute the root, then publish state atomically. A signed no-change heartbeat advances issuer-status observation only when it carries a newly authenticated source check. Root version and status-observation version are distinct. Development freshness timeout is a versioned **test policy**, initially 300 seconds; it is not an operational service level. Unknown, disabled, expired or stale issuer status pauses authorization. Currentness for execution is decided by the Part 5 program against its latest root and authenticated issuer-status policy at execution time; local transport data cannot authorize an older root.

## Witness, proof and private-state lifecycle

A witness context binds exact package/profile/setup/schema, private credential identity and version, action hash, issuer and policy version, registry root/version, mapping identity and local generation. M2's accepted v1 evaluator supports a single changed Merkle sibling with other credential/action inputs equal; it is an exact-output oracle for that shape only. No-op, multiple changes, new circuit, action, signature, certificate, issuer or policy changes route to validated full generation until individually qualified. A new circuit requires a new compiler-derived dependency mapping. Canonical WTNS and sorted sparse deltas are validated against the complete relation. Unsupported, cancelled, superseded or corrupt intermediate results never replace the installed context.

`ProofRequest` carries an immutable validated witness generation, authenticated target capability, package identity, action, deadline, mode and caller CSPRNG. `ProofResult` carries a complete ordinary Groth16 proof, ordered public inputs, exact package/VK/schema identity, root/version and committed cache generation. Full, Anchor, Dynamic and Selective modes may be exposed only after their exact relation and key pass independent verification; the final mode selector waits for accepted Phase 2 economics. New action starts with Full. A proof prepared for an older root may remain mathematically valid, but is never submitted as execution-current. New authenticated versions supersede pending work; cancellation/deadline/resource limits produce typed errors and leave no half-published generation.

Client states are import, bind action, obtain authenticated state, generate or refresh witness, prove, prepare submission, reconcile outcome, and cleanup. Revocation, expiry, stale issuer status, cancellation and retry exhaustion stop authorization. An uncertain transaction outcome must query the exact action receipt before any retry; a confirmed effect is never replayed. Retries coalesce to the newest authenticated state without delaying source publication.

Private cache envelopes retain exact context and authenticated generation, with publication before trusted receipt CAS as the existing engine requires. Key and receipt authority must survive restart independently of the attacker-replaceable cache to permit reuse. An ordinary file holding both cache and receipt cannot prevent whole-store rollback. The development default after process restart is `RebuildRequired`: reconcile fresh authenticated registry/status state and reconstruct with new entropy. A separately qualified independent receipt authority may enable durable reuse later; a process-local synthetic provider or same-store journal cannot claim it. Invalidation covers own revocation, action completion, logout, issuer disable/rotation, policy/profile change and key loss. Unknown CAS outcomes remain fenced until resolved. Cache bytes, keys, witnesses and blinders stay out of reports.

## Verification and application boundary

The chosen deployment path is a **matching versioned development key**, not original `.zkey` reuse. The local Solana adapter accepts typed ordinary proofs and their matching VK/package identity, validates canonical Fr/Fq bounds and curve points, and emits the existing 256-byte proof ABI: G1 A with y negated, G2 B coordinates in c1/c0 order, then G1 C, all 32-byte big-endian coordinates. It emits five 32-byte big-endian public scalars in the declared schema order. It rejects infinity, malformed/trailing data, wrong key/schema/package, wrong A convention and wrong Fq2 order. Full and refreshed proofs must pass both the matching ordinary verifier and an isolated local Solana verifier. Randomized proof bytes need not match.

Local component tests and an isolated verifier exercise these contracts. They do not execute the protected application effect. Part 5 modifies this repository's `program/src/lib.rs`, a versioned generated VK, `harness/src/solana.rs`, and the corresponding registry/prover/client entry points to add a program-owned work-request queue, exact account/effect reconstruction, strict current root, enabled issuer/policy/circuit, execution-time freshness, atomic effect plus nonce consumption, rollback on effect failure, and receipt reconciliation. No deployment or actual application integration occurs in this phase.
